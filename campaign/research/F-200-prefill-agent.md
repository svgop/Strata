# Research brief 5: prefill/TTFT (agent, 2026-10-15) — 10 findings

1. **Layered prefill** (arXiv:2510.08055 MLSys-2026; FlowPrefill arXiv:2602.16603) — vertical layer-group partitioning, interleave prefill+decode across groups: each weight touched ONCE per pass instead of per chunk. Chunked prefill inflates MoE expert loads **+39%**; layered cuts TTFT up to 70%, E2E 41%. Matches our GDN shape (recurrent state passes at layer-group boundaries). HIGHEST PRIORITY (= our T2.1, now sourced).
2. **Prompt-aware expert cache warming** (MoE-Infinity; MoEPic arXiv:2509.08342; llama.cpp #24528/#28248 ~63% cache ratio) — route the FIRST chunk, prefetch later chunks' hot experts while earlier layers compute. Low risk; complements 1.
3. **Green Contexts intra-GPU P/D disaggregation** (Nexus arXiv:2507.06608: 2.2× throughput, 20× TTFT vs vLLM; PegaInfer 5070 Ti +60%) — decode-kernel latency inflates up to 10× under chunked co-scheduling. CAVEATS: 5090 hits its POWER WALL with GC; L2 pollution mitigable ~10% with evict_first/.cs operators; runtime API since CUDA 13.1.
4. **libsmctrl TPC masking** (DuetServe) — per-stream SM masks at 2-SM granularity, no GC setup costs; 1.3× H100, −47% TTFT at 8×H100 scale. Works on consumer Ampere+; lighter than #3.
5. **Shortest-Prefill-First reordering** (LMCache blog; Nexus ablation: SPF alone cuts TTFT up to 90% under mixed load) — prefill length known at enqueue; trivial, pays when prompts queue.
6. **K-quant MMQ prefill rewrite** (llama.cpp WebGPU k-quant PR class: 1.33-3.78× prefill, no config change; turbo superblock variants) — if our IQ3_S prompt GEMMs are older MMQ-style, an MMA-idiomatic rewrite is a proven 2×+ play (= our T0.8 arm evidence base).
7. **sm_120 quant-GEMM status** (vLLM #35566; NVIDIA forums) — CUTLASS mm_fp4 wrong results + 99KiB smem carve bugs on SM120/121; Marlin-class weight-only-dequant is the reliable backend; hand-tuned SM120 GEMM reaches 129 TFLOPS. Template for large-M prefill GEMMs.
8. **GDN prefill must be CHUNKWISE** (sglang-jax #1416 → PR #1498) — per-token recurrent scan of the gated delta rule = O(T) serial small ops = the dominant long-context prefill cost; fix = chunkwise-parallel (dense matmuls + small triangular solve, chunk-boundary states in HBM). VERIFY OURS; also verify 32k chunk boundaries persist/reload GDN states (PLE state + QSA index caches are radix-cache compatible per LMSYS Qwen-Flash-Next blog).
9. **CacheBlend family** (arXiv:2405.16444 EuroSys'25 best paper; CacheClip 2510.10129; InfoBlend) — non-prefix KV reuse with selective recompute: ~3× TTFT for RAG/multi-chunk prompts; zero for one-off unique prompts. Conditional.
10. **Chunk shaping** (DuetServe; arXiv:2504.14489; DistServe; LMSYS chunked-pipeline) — fixed token budgets fail as context grows (attention share rises); on an IDLE GPU keep chunks LARGE (amortize the +39% expert reloads); when co-running, budget by predicted ms; 10-25% TTFT from shaping alone.

**Cross-cutting: 3/4 (overlap) isolate compute only — L2/DRAM + power wall remain; pair with evict_first mitigation.**
