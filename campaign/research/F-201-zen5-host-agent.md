# Deep brief 3: Zen5 host-side (investigative-researcher, 2026-10-15) — 10 findings

1. **CCD placement**: cross-CCD transfer ~200ns on Zen5 desktop (2.5× worse than Zen4; both L3s + 2GHz IF, per-CCD 64/32 GB/s); V-cache CCD boosts ~5.2 vs 5.7 GHz. Split: serving loop + doorbell pollers on CCD0 (V-cache, L3-resident flags ~50cy), 8 heavy GEMV workers on CCD1 @5.7GHz (DRAM-streaming, cache-agnostic); PER-CCD queues, single cross-CCD completion flag; never let a hot flag line live on both CCDs. 2 bounces/expert-call ≈ 400-800ns fabric transit.
2. **PAUSE = ~65 cycles on Zen5** (Agner, 9800X3D-measured) — the "slower on newer parts" lore is Intel/old-AMD; full-rate spin fine to ~2-4µs, then exponential PAUSE backoff, then nanosleep.
3. **Locked RMWs + MFENCE got 2-3× costlier on Zen5** (LOCK ~15-20cy vs 8; MFENCE 82cy vs 60; SFENCE 0.5cy, LFENCE 1cy) — de-lock flag protocols: plain store + SFENCE release, load acquire. Purge LOCK/MFENCE from the admission waves + dispatch paths.
4. **Zen5 = TRUE 512-bit machine** (2×512b FMA, 2×512b loads/cy, VPERMT2B 0.5cy, no AVX-512 freq offset): dequant GEMV wants shuffle-LUT (VPERMT2B), 8 independent accumulator chains (every SIMD op is 2-cycle latency now), 512b loads/256b stores, NO gathers (4-13cy). Warm experts ~2× Zen4; cold experts DRAM-bound regardless. AVX-512 boost recovery >100ms after bursts — sustained bursts amortize better.
5. **VNNI 0.5cy/zmm = 128 int8 MACs/cy** — pays only at batch≥2 with repacked int8 rows; IQ3_S unpack competes. DEFER unless hot experts requant to int8 tiles.
6. **Hugepage the 40GB pool**: 4KB = 10.5M IOMMU PTEs (~40MB page tables) vs 20,480 at 2MB; IOTLB miss ~330ns (Farshin, PeerJ CS 2023; up to 20% throughput loss at 4KB, recovers ~5% at 2MB). RESERVE 2MB pages (hugetlbfs/hugepages-2048kB) + cudaHostRegister — madvise THP cannot collapse after pinning. +5-15% on the 12.7GB/s staging path.
7. **UMWAIT/UMONITOR do NOT exist on AMD** (WAITPKG is Intel-only; MONITORX CPL0) — admission waits must be timer-based. REJECT.
8. **RDTSC ~38cy (cheaper), lfence;rdtsc ~8ns serialized, no kernel entry; vDSO ~20-30ns** — time checks once per backoff doubling, not per poll.
9. **sched_yield measured-useless** (mean 857ns, max 11.32ms tail) — graded backoff instead; consolidate the 14-30 cudaDeviceSynchronize admission waves into event waits (cudaStreamWaitEvent/cuStreamWaitValue32) — hundreds of µs/fence + removes GPU-side spin thread (measure per-call with rdtsc first).
10. **DRAM reality**: ~70ns latency, ~60-70GB/s real (not 96), ~4GB/s/core with 15 workers; IQ3_S cold GEMV ≈ bytes/4GB-per-core — prefetch/overlap beats kernel width; V-cache covers ~0.24% of 40GB (hot experts only).

**Priority: (1) hugepage+re-register pool+rings, (2) CCD split re-pin + per-CCD queues, (3) strip LOCK/MFENCE, (4) shuffle-LUT 512b dequant w/ 8 chains, (5) graded backoff (no yield, no UMWAIT), (6) fence consolidation after rdtsc measurement. Expected: staging → ~14GB/s class, fence host-component deleted, flag-variance collapsed.**
