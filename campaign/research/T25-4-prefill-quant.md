## Summary

The four repos divide cleanly. **basalt** rebuilt the prefill expert path around an extracted llama.cpp-MMQ (pin `3cf0325`) with per-format tile loaders (14 types incl. NVFP4-in-MMQ and a 6-bit Q6_K loader), a chunk-size-adaptive expert stream (staging ring under 6,144-token chunks, fixed CPU share above), a TC chunked-GDN prefill, and a single-file `.basalt` pack (2 MiB-aligned sections, per-section SHA-256, atomic `.partial` writes) whose converter quantizes dense **once from BF16** with a per-tensor distance gate. **maya** owns the quant *methodology*: per-expert GPTQ with error feedback through llama.cpp's own per-block quantizer, measured per-(tensor,type) error tables on ~32-expert samples, regex-recipe assembly, and a published quality ladder (Maya-S24 97.7% → Maya-S 97.9% → Maya-M 97.9% → Maya-L 99.2% zero-shot retention vs FP8; KL 0.428 → 0.188). **Xolotl** owns the *screening harness* (quant_quality.py incl. the prompt-path `prompt`/`pcompare` scorers — already byte-identical in OUR tree) and the deepest per-expert allocation machinery (v41: layer-streaming calib, FIT/held-out GPTQ screening, Lagrangian tier allocation with an unseen-expert floor, resumable per-layer chain, CUDA-graph GPTQ 8x). **Strata v0.2.1.4** turns out to be mostly *already ours*: the T20 wave merged its tools (only iq_pack.py differs materially — upstream added per-layer resumable experts.bin writes) and its prefill NVFP4/KQ stack (we carry W4A4 default + W4A8 TU + the strata_kq DSO + docs/QUANTS.md byte-identical to Xolotl's).

**The headline corrections to the assignment's framing:** (1) we do NOT lack "MMQ prefill" — we lack basalt's *unified* MMQ (Q6_K native without env, NVFP4 inside MMQ, DS4 fix) and their *stream调度* (staging ring, cpu-share), not the MMQ concept; (2) Xolotl's QUANTS.md + quant_quality.py are already in our tree — the actionable delta is *using* the un-armed `prompt`/`pcompare` legs in the re-quant canary; (3) the biggest transferable quant asset for the queued RECIPE-α is maya's finding that **GPTQ-on-down measured WORSE than plain imatrix rounding** (corroborating our Q2_0-down plan) while gate/up GPTQ + a Hessian prior for cold experts is where measured quality came from.
