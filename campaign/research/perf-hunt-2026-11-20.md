## 2. Suspect #1 — the expert-hit kernel's achieved bandwidth

**Derivation (mechanism, not accounting):** routed entries per solo window ≈ 1866-1882 VRAM;
unique experts per layer ≈ 12-16 (row reuse); unique expert bytes per window ≈ 48 layers ×
~13 experts × 2.66 MB ≈ **1.6 GB read in ~9 ms ≈ 185 GB/s achieved on a ~1790 GB/s part
(~10% of HBM peak)**. Marlin-class weight-only GEMV kernels reach 60-80% of peak. The gap is
the single largest priced term in the engine.

**Kernel forensics** (`src/kernels/cuda/s2_expert_grouped.cu:322-402`):
- One warp per (hit, row-pair); lane-strided 32-value chunk loop; weight codes loaded as 8 B
  `uint2` per lane — coalesced 256 B/warp/iteration (good); fp16 scales with 2:1 lane
  duplication (minor); activations broadcast via L1/L2 (good).
- Per 8 weight-bytes: `expand_codes` (IQ3_S bitfield unpack) + `chunk_s` (integer dot) + 2×
  fp32 FMA + fp16 decode. **Binding suspicion: integer-dequant ALU throughput per HBM byte** —
  the classic weight-only-quant GEMV wall llama.cpp hits without a Marlin/machete-class
  decompression. Classification pending the falsifier below (ALU-bound vs BW-bound vs
  occupancy).
- The gu→down intermediate passes through fp32 scratch + Q8_0 requant (3 kernels per layer
  per window): traffic ≈ 38 MB/window — 2% of the weight bytes, NOT the lever; the swiglu+q8
  fusion already took its share (upstream 0.2.10 port).
- Windows are CUDA-graphed (13 launches/window, not 2200) — launch overhead is NOT the lever.

**Falsifier (law 4, cheapest first — the instrument is MISSING, this is instrumentation debt
per law 11):** a standalone saturation microbench of `moe_hit_grouped_s2` (n_hits = full
cache, no doorbell, no window machinery) reporting GB/s. `bench/micro/` has
`moe_hit_parity.cu` (parity only — no timing). Decision table:
- ≥600 GB/s standalone → the kernel is fine; the live path is issue-rate/wait-bound → attack
  the doorbell/service structure instead (price collapses, suspect demoted).
- ~185 GB/s standalone → kernel-bound, the lever is real. Arms, in doctrine order
  (law 9: remove/fold/move before add): (a) pack-time or fill-time **layout conversion of the
  resident cache to a compute-friendly form** (the UD bf16-dense conversion is the in-tree
  precedent — same lever, already proven on another form class; VRAM cost priced against
  slot count, must hold 9600 slots or name the trade), (b) wider vector loads + `__ldg`
  pipelines + occupancy tuning inside the existing kernel, (c) L2 persistence window
  (`cudaAccessPolicyWindow`) over the resident cache span.
- PRICE at 500 GB/s: GPU term 9 → 3.2 ms, window 13.25 → ~7.7 ms ⇒ **M1 ×1.5-1.7**, bounded
  by the host floor (CPU experts + emit ≈ 4.2 ms) — S2 must fall with it or it becomes the
  new wall.
