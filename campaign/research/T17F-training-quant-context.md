# T17F — Training-side, quantization, and long-context levers beyond the queued FastMTP run

**Date:** 2026-10-17. **Mode:** read-only research (no builds, boots, API calls, git ops, training). **Scope:** the custom-2 M1-250 campaign, lane = training/quant/context (NOT kernels/scheduling — T13A/T15A/T15C own those). Excludes everything queued/closed: T14A arms 0-4 (recursion CE, LK overlap loss, AdaSPEC filtering, EAGLE-3 training-time test, the R2 post-cast QAT finetune), RT-r17 distill plateau, dense-MMQ prefill (F-230), GDN_CHUNKED, the T15B 262k test plan itself. This dossier only prices what is NOT already in those.

## 1. Executive summary

1. **The single biggest NEW lever is not training at all: a routing/importance-weighted re-quant of the expert pack (IQ3_S → ~3.0-3.2 bpw effective).** Decode is VRAM-hits-bound at 87-100% of the GDDR7 roofline and the cache is at its VRAM ceiling (SVG-CUSTOM-TODO.md:913-916, :912) — every byte shaved off the average expert blob buys BOTH bytes/token AND cache slots (+512 slots = +2.1pt hit, same file). The pack is ALREADY per-tensor mixed-format (native_experts.txt: IQ2_S/IQ3_XXS/IQ3_S gate-up + Q2_0/IQ4_NL down, SVG-CUSTOM-TODO.md:910-911), so a smarter chooser pass is a data artifact, not an engine project. Priced +6-10% M1 IF the quality gate holds; one-boot falsifier via the R5 G2 canary. The 2024-2026 literature (MxMoE, GEMQ, MoPEQ, Unsloth-dynamic, ik IQ_KS) says allocation beats uniform at exactly this bit band, and GEMQ names the exact hazard (router shift) plus the budget fix (router-only finetune, <0.04% of params).
2. **On drafter training, everything above the queued T14A arms collapses into two NEW candidates:** (A) on-policy rejection replay (Draft-OPD, arXiv 2605.29343) — our exact-match verifier already materializes every rejection + the target's pick, so the on-policy signal is free telemetry; (B) position-offset augmentation of the retrain dump (LongSpec's anchor-offset indices, arXiv 2502.17421) — the only credible answer to F-181's unseparated "filler-content vs rope-position" acceptance decay before the 262k switch. Data scaling beyond the planned 8-16M MIMIC tokens is a third-order term (+1-3pp at best).
3. **KV below int8 is a DOESN'T for decode speed on this engine:** KV reads per token are indexer-bounded (≤2051 cells × 12 QSA layers) + fixed-size GDN state, so KV bandwidth is not on the decode critical path (T12/T15B); the q4-KV literature's speedups live in memory-bound batch regimes we are not in, and llama.cpp q4_0 KV can even be kernel-slower at depth (NVIDIA forum bench). The only real payoff is host-side: pinned KV 3.09 → ~1.7 GiB at 262k — and host RAM is not binding (171.6 GiB, T15B:73). Park unless the 262k lane ever hits RAM/PCIe walls.
4. **The 262k switch needs no new mechanism — it needs the retrain dump to see deep positions (item 2B) and one real-corpus acceptance probe.** F-181's acceptance cliff (62.4% cold @157k vs 79.4% warm @244k) is confounded by synthetic filler; a depth-lane run on real text is the cheapest falsifier on this entire board and gates whether ANY training intervention is warranted. MagicDec's bottleneck-shift argument plus our own 173.9 t/s @244k measurement say speculative decoding keeps paying at depth — no structural reason to expect the switch to regress acceptance if the pack and positions are held fixed.
5. **Exact-match coupling is a moat, not a constraint, for everything above:** rejection telemetry, drafter swaps (lossless by verification), and pack requants (drafter sees the same backbone) all compose. The two coupling-derived laws stand: F-230 (asymmetric prefill-bit changes pay ~−2.5% decode) prices any prefill-path numerics change; any KV-bit change is a full different-bits adoption (G2/G3 + acceptance A/B).

## 3. Deep dives

### 3.1 Drafter training beyond the queued run (lane a)

T14A arms 0-4 already own: recursive teacher-forced CE (β=0.8, K=6-8), LK acceptance-overlap loss, AdaSPEC selective KD, EAGLE-3 training-time test, and the R2 post-cast QAT finetune (T14A:154-159). What is genuinely NEW from this sweep:

**On-policy rejection replay (Draft-OPD, S5).** Draft-OPD's diagnosis: SFT-on-target-states drafters plateau because of an offline-to-inference mismatch — trained on target states, evaluated on draft-induced states; their fix trains on target-supervised feedback at positions the DRAFTER induced, mixing accepted and rejected proposals (+23% over EAGLE-3, +13% over DFlash). For us the mechanism is unusually cheap: exact-match verification is a free, dense teacher. Every rejected draft position comes with the target's actual pick (the rescue path materializes it), the coupled sampler knows the draw, and the M3d counters already bucket by depth (T14C:55-59). The candidate is therefore NOT full Draft-OPD (their target-assisted rollout machinery is EAGLE/DFlash-specific); it is the replayable subset: during the MIMIC-corpus harvest (T14A Arm 0.3), ALSO run the drafter and record (position, draft token, accepted/rejected, target top-pick) tuples; upweight rejected positions ~2-4x in the Arm-1 CE. T14A already planned the verifier p-list dump for Arm 2 (T14A:115) — the rejection tuples ride the same dump at near-zero extra cost. Verdict: **APPLIES** — composes with the queued run as a data-arm, no architecture change, lossless by verification.

**Position-offset augmentation (LongSpec, S23/S41) — assigned to lane (d) below because its payoff is the 262k switch.**

**Data scaling (S1, S6, S30).** The scaling evidence is weaker than folklore: FastMTP's 389K samples were their FULL corpus (no ablation curve published at smaller sizes); Red Hat's recipe banks usable deltas from ~5K conversations (T11.R3 read); "Decoding Speculative Decoding" (S6) shows drafting capability is causally driven by draft training data but their 111%-throughput draft was a from-scratch architecture swap, not a data-curve on a fixed head. EAGLE-3's "8x data" (+20-40% over EAGLE-2) is confounded with its feature-fusion input (T14A:118). On OUR head the marginal curve is unknown; the plan's 8-16M token floor is reasonable, and the first doubling is the only step worth pre-pricing. Verdict: **PARTIAL** — one 2x arm max, gated on items above leaving measurable headroom.

**Quantization-aware draft training.** Already banked as T14A risk R2 (T14A:156): direct-cast Q4 with per-tensor AMAX recalibration, and a 1-epoch LR 5e-6 QAT finetune ONLY if the cast costs >2pp acceptance. The new literature merely corroborates the default: ML-SpecQD (S8) ships direct-cast (BF16→MXFP4, "no draft pre-training or alignment") as a production recipe at up to 2x; QSpec (S7) needs no retraining at all for its quantized drafting path. No change to the queued plan. Note also: the drafter's routed experts ALREADY serve at Q2_0 and the head at Q4 (custom-iq3_s-8081.json --mtp-q4; SVG-CUSTOM-TODO.md:956-958), and T11.R3's harvest trains on quantized-backbone hiddens — the "draft natively models the quantized backbone" goal is already structurally met by the queued recipe; the residual exposure is only the post-cast shift of the head's OWN tensors, which R2 owns.

### 3.2 Backbone requant / mixed-quant toward Q2_0 speed at IQ3_S quality (lane b)

**The economics are slots, not just bytes.** Standing regime: 9,337 slots / 17.71 GiB = 38% of the 24,576 gate-up+down pairs resident; the hits line runs at 87-100% of bandwidth; "50.3GB of pairs > 32GB card; every extra GiB = +512 slots = +2.1pt" (SVG-CUSTOM-TODO.md:912-916). Average blob 2.046 MB at ~3.48 effective bpw (4.92M params/pair, SVG-CUSTOM-TODO.md:910-911). Q2_0 (2.5625 bpw) would be 1.57 MB — the fast lane's M1 242.6 vs IQ3_S 198.5 (F-234a addendum 2, SVG-CUSTOM.md:8064-8072) is exactly this bytes+slots gap plus its acceptance profile. A chooser pass targeting ~3.0-3.2 bpw average (−8-13% blob) moves BOTH terms in our favor.

**The mechanism is GGUF-native and already half-in-tree.** The pack is per-tensor mixed-format TODAY (native_experts.txt types: IQ2_S/IQ3_XXS/IQ3_S gate-up + Q2_0/IQ4_NL down, L47 IQ4_XS; SVG-CUSTOM-TODO.md:910-911) — i.e., the engine consumes format heterogeneity per tensor natively and the previous quant pass already made per-tensor choices. Three external results say the CHOOSER is where the quality lives at 3 bits:
- **MxMoE (S10):** linear-BLOCK-level (gate/up/down) ILP allocation with roofline-aware runtime estimates; at 2.25-3.25 bpw it beats uniform GPTQ by up to 2.4 WikiText2 ppl and beats uniform-8B accuracy-equivalent by up to 29% speed; finds down_proj systematically needs more precision than gate/up — independently confirmed by our own ffn_down super-weight finding (S-source: arXiv 2505.02390, banked T7.C.4, SVG-CUSTOM-TODO.md:369-372).
- **GEMQ (S11):** global (not per-layer) expert bit allocation via Taylor/Fisher importance; documents the hazard that matters most to us — at 1.5-bit, >40% of tokens ROUTE TO DIFFERENT EXPERTS; mitigation = finetune only the router (<0.04% params, minutes) on quantized weights, recovering most drift; warns calibration domain must match the task mix (their GSM8K collapse under C4-only calibration maps to our mimic/MMLU mix).
- **MoPEQ / MoEQuant / MoQE (S12, S13, S14):** expert-level mixed precision from activation-frequency and Hessian-trace sensitivity; we already possess the frequency side as the learned expert profile + /metrics routing counters.
- **Unsloth dynamic 2.0/3.0 (S42):** the deployed-GGUF proof that per-tensor bit allocation at fixed total size beats uniform quants on benchmarks; **imatrix (S37):** the GGUF-native importance weighting — and our calibration corpus should be OUR serving mix, not wiki.
- **ik IQ_K/IQ_KS series (S36, S45):** the newer i-quant family (IQ2_K…IQ4_KS) is being merged toward llama.cpp mainline (jangwook, 2026-02) — a second-generation option, but it needs new kernels in OUR tree (the ik formats are not in our type set), so it is phase 2, only if phase 1's chooser pass over EXISTING types saturates.

**Pricing item 1.** Blob 2.046 → 1.90 MB (−7%, ~3.2 bpw): hits-line headroom +7%; slots 9,337 → ~9,990 (+653 → +2.7pt hit → −~36% miss bytes at 7% miss, T7.C.3 logic SVG-CUSTOM-TODO.md:367-368). Compounded (multiplicative on different terms, sharing only the window): **+6-10% M1** central; conc benefits most (miss-exposure highest there, F-183's ±500-slot = ±4.9% conc band). At the aggressive end (~2.9 bpw, −13%) the arithmetic reaches +10-14% but the quality gate becomes the whole question: GEMQ's 2.5-bit Mixtral lost ~7pp MMLU even with allocation — our bar is MMLU 95.0/mimic 87.9 anchors, so the 3.2-bpw first pack is the right risk posture.

**Gate class.** A new pack changes ALL bits coherently (prefill and decode together), so F-230's asymmetric-prefill tax does not apply as stated — but its MECHANISM does: prefill logits shift → coupled exact-match acceptance baseline shifts → price acceptance vs the old pack on the same traffic (the M3b canary + acceptance accounting), plus the standard G2/G3. The drafter sees the same shifted backbone (it is coupled), so no draft/target mismatch arises — this is unlike the dense-MMQ case where only prefill bits moved. Router shift (GEMQ) is the specific new hazard; the learned expert profile (profile-save-every) and the adapt tier interact with routing — re-verify ROUTE_RESIDENT/adapt behavior on the new pack in the same boot.

**Effort/falsifier.** Zero GPU training: imatrix sweep over the MIMIC corpus + quantize pass over the 85 GB pack on the 9950X3D (~1-2 days, tools exist in the llama.cpp ecosystem; our fork's quant chooser is upstream-derived). Cheapest falsifier: quantize ONE 12-layer band's experts first, boot it (mixed packs are already legal — per-tensor types), run the R5 G2 logit canary + M3b in one boot. No engine code, fully reversible (pack swap).

### 3.3 KV below int8 (lane c)

The tempting literature number — KIVI's 2.35-3.47x throughput (S17) — comes from memory-bound, large-batch, long-context regimes. Our decode window's KV traffic is structurally capped: QSA layers read ≤2051 selected cells/layer (idx_top_k + block − 1, T15B:25) and GDN layers carry fixed-size recurrence state; the T12-MEM audit found decode decay with depth is staging-SYNC, not KV bandwidth (SVG-CUSTOM-TODO.md:932-934), and the dominant line is expert hits. So:
- **Decode speed: ~0-2%.** DOESN'T as a throughput lever.
- **Host-side at 262k: pinned KV 3.09 → ~1.7 GiB and streamed prefill bytes −50%** (12,684 B/cell, T15B:72-73) — real, but MemAvailable is 171.6 GiB (T15B:73) and PCIe share is ~0.3-0.4% (T12-MEM item 6). A solution to a problem we do not have.
- **Quality/kernel risk is asymmetric:** community + vendor data agree q8_0 KV is near-lossless while q4_0 measurably hurts and can be KERNEL-slower at depth (NVIDIA DGX Spark bench: q4_0 ~92% slower at 64k; asymmetric q8K/q4V preferred — S38, S43, S20); the eddoursel fork's k8v4 (INT8 K + Hadamard-rotated Q4_0 V) is the in-family precedent (SVG-CUSTOM-TODO.md:517-519). Under exact-match verify, ANY KV-bit change alters target logits → output changes (full G2/G3 class) and borderline accept/reject decisions flip (the F-230 coupling mechanism, now decode-side).
- **The research recipes that make low-bit KV survive (KVQuant pre-RoPE per-channel non-uniform, KIVI per-channel-K/per-token-V, ZipCache salience, S18/S17/S19) all assume control of the attention kernel.** Our QSA selection + custom KV path makes each an engine project. Verdict: **PARK**; re-price only if the 262k lane ever binds on pinned RAM or PCIe. If it is ever run: asymmetric q8K/q4V with the V-side rotation, judged by depth-lane M3b + G2, one boot.

### 3.4 Long context: making acceptance hold at 240k+ and landing 262k (lane d)

**What the ladder actually measured (F-181, SVG-CUSTOM.md:5828-5851):** 262k native warm 244k depth: 173.9 t/s, accept 79.4%; 1M YaRN 937k: 98.7 t/s, accept 60.9%; cold 157k: 62.4% (short sample); YaRN ≈ −4-8% prefill at matched depth; "how much is filler-content vs rope-extended positions is NOT separated." T15B's test plan already gates the switch on 3v3 + depth-lane acceptance ≥ ~75% + MMLU (T15B:121-129). Three NEW external findings complete the picture:

1. **Position-side training fix (LongSpec, S23):** their drafter holds acceptance at 128k-class depths because of two training tricks — Anchor-Offset Indices (train with large random position offsets so deep positions are seen on short data) and Flash Noisy Training (+14.7% acceptance length). Mapped to our queued retrain: the MTP head is position-shared and was pretrained ≤131k-equivalent positions; adding offset-augmented lanes to the T14A Arm 0.3 dump is ~zero marginal cost and is the only mechanism identified anywhere that targets the head's deep-position behavior. This is item 3 in the table.
2. **Spec keeps paying at depth (MagicDec, S21):** the bottleneck shifts from weights to KV/state as batch×context grows, which is the regime where speculative decoding helps THROUGHPUT, not just latency; our own measurement (173.9 t/s @244k warm, acceptance 79.4%) is the direct proof on this engine. There is no structural argument for disabling spec on the 262k lane. Caveat from LongSpec's own comparisons: PLD and MagicDec-style sparse-KV drafting show NEGATIVE acceleration at low batch — irrelevant to us because our drafter is the native coupled head (no separate draft KV), which is exactly the LongSpec-style "zero extra draft storage" design point.
3. **Chunked prefill × MTP is a live production interaction (vLLM Qwen3-Next recipe, S40):** the official recipe ships `--no-enable-chunked-prefill` WITH the MTP config — production engines still trip over this interaction. Ours is architecturally safer (prefill windows are separate from decode windows; cohorts park, not interleave), GDN_CHUNKED is adopted decode-neutral (F-224), and the queued FUSE_WAIT/DMA_BATCH items own the measured depth decay (staging-sync, SVG-CUSTOM-TODO.md:932-934). Action: none beyond T15B's plan; watch the depth ladder's per-chunk timing for the staging term, not a bit-level term.

**KV layout for 262k:** T15B covers it (banked): pinned KV linear at 12,684 B/cell, streamed cells 229,376/layer, kv-resident 32768 unchanged, kvg_ensure stays off. The only external addition worth recording: KVQuant's per-layer sensitivity allocation (S18) is the correct recipe IF 3.3's park ever lifts. YaRN itself is clean at short prompts on this model (MMLU 94.29 under the 1M config, SVG-CUSTOM.md:5848-5850) — no action.

### 3.5 Composers with exact-match coupled MTP (lane e)

- **Rejection telemetry → training (item 2)** and **acceptance-probability head → gate policy (item 5)** are the two composers that turn the coupled verifier into a sensor. For us it would consume the M3d counters' supervised signal; the engine-side consumer (draft_policy / spec-min-p) is M-class surgery and is NOT recommended before the retrain lands (T14C's gates G4/G5 already re-open depth questions on the retrained drafter for one boot each).

## 4. Contradictions and their resolution

1. **"Nothing dominates IQ3_S per-bit at bs1 on sm_120" (T7.C verdict, SVG-CUSTOM-TODO.md:352-354) vs item 1's +6-10%.** Not a contradiction: T7.C evaluated FOREIGN format families at fixed bpw (XFP/NVFP4/Marlin — every tensor-core format costs MORE bytes at M=1) and rejected 4bpw+ requants (+25-30% bytes). Item 1 goes the OTHER direction (fewer bytes on insensitive tensors within EXISTING formats) and monetizes the slot term T7.C's per-bit framing ignored. The slot term is measured, not modeled: +512 slots = +2.1pt hit (SVG-CUSTOM-TODO.md:912-916), F-183's slot-band sensitivity.
2. **KIVI/KVQuant "2-4-bit KV with negligible quality loss" vs our PARK verdict.** Their quality claims come with per-channel/non-uniform kernels and dense-attention models; our QSA indexer caps KV reads so the SPEED motive is absent, and the quality risk under exact-match coupling is categorically different (acceptance flips, not just perplexity). Resolution: park on speed grounds; the quality literature is recorded for the day RAM binds.
3. **Draft-OPD's +23% over EAGLE-3 vs T14C's ceiling of ~222-229 M1 for the retrain alone.** Different baselines: Draft-OPD improves drafter TRAINING over strong SFT baselines on EAGLE-3/DFlash architectures; T14C's ceiling is OUR fixed-shape acceptance arithmetic. The replay arm moves marginals INSIDE the T14C model (scenario (a) terms); it does not change c_row. No conflict; both numbers stand.
4. **"Down_proj needs more precision" (MxMoE S10) vs the requint's byte-reduction goal (which would prefer down at Q2_0 as today).** Resolution: the pack ALREADY spends bits asymmetrically (gate-up at 2-3 bit i-quants, down at Q2_0/IQ4_NL) — MxMoE's allocation says the budget may be MISALLOCATED in the other direction for OUR model; the chooser pass re-derives it from measured sensitivity instead of inheriting the upstream default. The G2 canary arbitrates.
5. **vLLM disables chunked prefill with MTP; we run chunked prefill (auto:32768) with MTP.** Different engines: vLLM co-batches prefill chunks with decodes in one step (Sarathi-style), where MTP rows collide with prefill chunks; our prefill and decode are separate window types. The citation is recorded as an interaction warning, not a defect in our config.
