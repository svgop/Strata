## 1. Summary

**Upstream is empty; basalt is the only kernel donor; Xolotl and Maya are format/model-specific; DeepInfer has no readable decode kernels.**

1. **Upstream Strata v0.2.1.4 has literally nothing we lack**: `git log v0.2.1..v0.2.1.4 -- src/kernels/cuda src/prefill` is empty, and our tree differs from the v0.2.1.4 checkout in exactly four kernel files (elementwise, fused_gr, sampler, verify_kernels) — all four diffs are OUR adoptions (COHORTS, norm_fast/S3, T18 guards). Upstream main (pushed 2026-10-20, ahead of the pin) is out of scope per the task.
2. **basalt's decode window is a different machine built on our exact pack formats.** The decisive new fact of this study: basalt's device-planned MoE tail (`dec_moe` route/glue/wait/combine), its two-row gate/up (`gu2`), its fused GDN recurrence (`dec_gdn_rec`), its fused QSA per-token kernels (`qsa_dec_fused`) and its `dense_tc` int8-TC path **all dispatch on our IQ3_S pack without format changes** — our expert pairs (gate/up types 18=IQ3_XXS, 21=IQ3_S, 22=IQ2_S × down 20=IQ4_NL, 42=Q2_0) are in `dec_moe`'s supported pair list verbatim, our n_ff=640 matches their hard gate, and Q8_0 (our dense projections) was added to `dense_tc` at 6e53eee6. T23's "matches their device-plan MoE skeleton (shared parent)" is **corrected**: the *plan* skeleton (device residency tables) is shared, but the *execution* path that replaces our grouped-matvec + copy_rows + moe_hit_add + separate combine + flag-spin waits with a route kernel + in-launch planning + in-kernel tier wait + folded combine is basalt-only and unranked.
3. **The GDN-middle fold ladder has a rung T23 missed and a blocker T23 didn't know.** `dec_gdn_rec` (conv+gates+recurrence+gated-norm+q8_1 in one cluster launch, bitwise, no weight-type restriction) runs on our pack today; `mid_chain` (T23 #4, projections+recurrence in one persistent launch) **does not** — its `type_ok` accepts {12 Q4_K, 13 Q5_K, 14 Q6_K, 21 IQ3_S, 23 IQ4_XS} per projection and our GDN qkv/z/out are all **Q8_0 (type 8)** (`mid_chain.cu:444`, `unified-kernel.md:12`, our pack index type column 8). mid_chain on our pack requires the Q6_K-dense re-quant first (the specialised `(14,14,14)` triple exists) or a new Q8_0 instance.
4. **Our cheapest hc-line falsifier is in-tree and un-armed.** Our fork already carries the S23/S25 `STRATA_HC_Q8` arm (fused_gr.cu:85,1705; verify.cpp:2223; generate.cpp:3783) and the S26 `tsum` warp-sum (s26_tsum.cuh, included by fused_gr.cu:11) — T15A/T15C record them as never A/B'd. That prices the hc8 class (kill the unpack, fewer bytes/row) with one env boot before any port of basalt's `hc8_gr`/`hc_fused`.
5. Everything else basalt has that we lack is small-but-bitwise: sampler one-launch split, QSA occupancy 2/SM, draft-head single-launch argmax+prob, commit finisher, 16-byte dequant stores, per-device smem opt-in, hot embedding rows. And basalt's own graveyard (whole-layer persistent kernel retired slower; `pk_moe` persistent MoE and `dec_shx` one-launch shared expert built and parity-tested but **not dispatched** at the pin; five hc experiment knobs measured neutral and dropped) is as informative as its shipped code.

Ranked answer in one line: **the two T23 PORT-NOWs stand (now with cheaper falsifiers and one blocker removed), the unranked `dec_moe` tail becomes the #3 port, `dec_gdn_rec` becomes the bitwise rung that de-risks T23's #4, and the rest is a bitwise small-tranche plus a large negative-findings ledger.**

## 4. Per-candidate notes (both-tree anchors; new material only — T23 #1-#6 details are in that file)

### 3. dec_moe — the unranked MoE tail (the study's principal new finding)
- **Their side.** `dm_init` runs unconditionally at verifier setup and prints the reason when it stands down (`verify.cpp:475-497`); stand-down reasons include non-native packs, layer splits, `--remote-expert-opt`, all-resident, window > kDmCap=128, or any layer failing `dec_moe_formats_ok`. `dec_moe_formats_ok` (`dec_moe.cuh:1882-1904`) requires the (gate/up, down) pair ∈ `DM_PAIRS` (`:1024-1027`) and `n_ff == 640 && n_embd % 256 == 0 && !g_old_kernels && !g_grouped_v1`. **Our pack's six pairs — (18,20) (21,20) (22,20) (18,42) (21,42) (22,42) — are all in the list; our n_ff is 640.** The window per layer: route kernel (`verify.cpp:1943`) → glue (`:2045`, when `dec_moe_glue_ok`, default since `g_gu2=2` at `iq_kernels.cu:2542` and `dm_->vram=-1` at `:515`) or the TMA-pipeline `dec_moe_vram` (`:2047`, **not reachable by default**) or plain `native_expert_grouped` (`:2050`) → `dec_moe_wait` (`:2105`, PDL scope) → `dec_moe_combine` as its programmatic dependent (`:2109`). The wait kernel carries the tier stamps (S[0] doorbell, S[1] CUDA0 done, S[2] peer landed, S[3] all tiers, S[4] CPU pool landed; `dec_moe.cuh:16-38`) — a ready-made instrument for pricing exactly our 3h flag-spin class. The CPU pool stays host-served as before; a layer with no CPU entries never waits on it.
- **gu2, the inner kernel** (`gu2.cuh:1-13`): grouped gate/up of the grid formats (IQ2_XXS…IQ3_S, Q2_0) at two rows per warp — the third pass runs row A on lanes 0-15 and row B on 16-31; weights via aligned 32-bit loads + `__funnelshift_r`, sign masks via `prmt` sign-replicating mode, grid table in shared memory. **Bitwise** native_gu_multi. Standalone value on our card is near zero (expert hits are 87-92% of GDDR7 roofline, byte-floored — T13A 3c); its value is instruction-count and latency inside the glue's PDL chain (GU2=2 makes SwiGLU/down programmatic dependents with down-rows prefetched to L2 pre-wait, 1efad92a; ≤24 block rows with group stride, 01d78947).
- **What it replaces on our side** (both-tree anchors): our `resident_plan`/`doorbell_publish_res` (`verify.cpp:1558-1590`), tier waits `waitA/waitB/waitCPU` (`:1685-1699,1714-1719`), always-on PCIe grouped + `copy_rows*` + `moe_hit_add` (`:1700-1736`), separate combine (`:1743-1763`). Their `dec_moe.hpp:5-14` claims per-tier bitwise vs `native_expert_grouped`; "only the PCIe share is gone (those entries go to the CPU pool)" — for us (no peer) res1 is the no-peer path.
- **Parked relatives (negative findings):** `pk_moe` — one persistent launch for the whole routed tail incl. shared fold, parity-tested, "leaves an eighth of the SMs to other streams" (`pk_moe.hpp:1-45`) — has **no call site** at fb0944c7 (grep over src/: only its definition). `dec_shx` one-launch shared expert (662f594f) likewise undispached; the shipped shared path is `shared_expert_multi` on a side-stream fork (`verify.cpp:1976-1990`). The retired full-layer kernel's post-mortem (`unified-kernel.md:24-56`) explains why: cross-CTA dependency latency (58 vs 30 µs byte floor at T=3), instruction-fetch stalls on 1.5 MB SASS, harness/engine mismatch 0.1-0.3.

### 4. dec_gdn_rec — the bitwise rung of the GDN-middle fold
- Fuses exactly the three kernels our window runs per GDN layer after the projections: our anchors `verify.cpp` rec/conv/ab/z classes inside 3b (T13A:13); their fused file declares the replaced set in its header comment (`dec_gdn_rec.cu:3-6`). Cluster of four blocks per value head; rank owns 32 state columns; RMS-norm butterflies through DSMEM in rank order; alpha from rank 0, beta rank 1. State and constants load **before** the grid-dependency wait ("the window never writes the state") — a sanctioned PDL preamble. `dec_gdnf_mode()` is `constexpr 3` (`dec_gdn.hpp:59`): split kernel + z forked beside. Segmented variant (`dec_gdn_rec_seg`) covers batched/segmented windows bitwise.
- Ladder to T23 #4: dense_tc_pair(qkv+z, one launch, Q8_0-capable, `verify.cpp:1483-1491`) + dec_gdn_rec + dense_tc(out) = three launches ≈ mid_chain's four-in-one minus the persistence. Only the persistent version needs `type_ok` types.

### 5. QSA decode family
- Basalt rewrote decode attention into `qsa_dec_attn.cu` (new file; CHUNK=64 cells/block, G=12 query heads/KV head, staged chunks, gate in merge, width-dependent tiles/CTA) while keeping upstream's `qsa_decode_attn.cu` for fallback/batch — the 1,047-line "diff" on the shared file is largely drift plus their early-trigger/PDL insertions. Our tree is upstream v0.2.1's continuation (attn_chunk_q8_v3 etc.), which basalt does not have. ## 8. Implications

- **Sequencing:** the hc falsifier (S23/S25 env boot) and the head microbench (mmvq vs mmvq_il vs dense_tc shapes) cost no code and gate the two PORT-NOW ports. dense_tc's Q8_0 support means the port can land **before** any re-quant decision, and the Q6_K-dense re-quant becomes a pure follow-on win on top of it (as T25-4 already priced).
- **The window architecture gap is one item, not many:** dec_moe (#3) + dec_gdn_rec (#4) + qsa_dec_fused (#5) + the finisher (#8) are all the same project — converting our ~2,050-node, flag-spin-coordinated window into their fewer-launch, PDL-chained, device-waited window. T23's #3 (PDL) and #4 (mid_chain) are members of this set, not separate gambits; the effort estimate should be shared, not per-item.
- **Bitwise discipline is portable and load-bearing:** nearly every basalt kernel above ships with a parity selftest and a "same sums in the same order" header contract. Adopting the parity-harness pattern (candidate 9's discipline) is a precondition for the tranche, per their own Lessons (standalone harnesses misled at ratio 0.1-0.3 unless they reproduce engine start state).
- **The negative ledger saves boots:** pk_moe, dec_shx, dec_moe_vram, the hc knob family, and the whole-layer kernel are already-explored dead ends or parked branches — none should be independently re-derived in our campaign.

## 10. Methods note

Diff matrices were computed three ways (ours-vs-X, basalt-vs-upstream, Xolotl/Maya-vs-upstream) so every shared-file diff could be attributed; ours==upstream held for all but four files, which made basalt-vs-upstream ≡ basalt-vs-ours and let basalt's own commits (checked `in-upstream=NO` per sha) attribute each change. Defaults were established from code (`g_gu2=2`, `d->vram=-1`, `dec_gdnf_mode()=3`, dm unconditional, no `getenv` in verify.cpp), not docs. Unproductive searches, so they are not repeated: (1) per-file `grep '^\+.*//'` with BRE `\+` (quantifier, not literal) returned empty once — redone with `-E '^[+]'`; (2) pk_moe/dec_shx dispatch hunt across src/core+src/program — no call sites exist (that IS the finding); (3) `dm_` env parsing in verify.cpp — none; gating is structural in dm_init; (4) Maya s2_expert_grouped 600-line diff inspected only by header — it is their GLM-FFN variant of the S2 grouped kernel, model-bound, not diffed line-by-line; (5) DeepInfer vendor/ submodules are unfetched by design — no kernel claims made about them. No builds, boots, or GPU use; nothing written outside this file and the INDEX row; all reference clones untouched (read-only study copies per MANIFEST).
