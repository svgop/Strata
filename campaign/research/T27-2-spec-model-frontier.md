## (a) Frontier map, with sources + dates

### A. Training-side (T14B rider class) — the live frontier for us

**A1. EAGLE 3.1 — "attention drift" diagnosis + two fixes (May 26, 2026).**
Joint EAGLE/vLLM/TorchSpec release. Root cause of deep-position acceptance decay named **attention drift**: as speculation deepens, the drafter's fused inputs go imbalanced (hidden-state magnitudes grow) and its focus shifts from sink tokens to its own generated tokens. Fix 1: **FC normalization** — normalize each target hidden state after injection, before the FC fusion. Fix 2: **post-norm hidden-state feedback** — feed the post-norm hidden back into the next drafter step so the drafter behaves like a recursive invocation rather than stacked extra layers. Reported: **up to 2× acceptance length vs EAGLE-3 on long-context**; 2.03× per-user throughput at C=1 on Kimi-K2.6-NVFP4 (GB200, TP=4) [donor-reported]. Training in TorchSpec PR #97; vLLM PR #42764 (config-driven, EAGLE-3-checkpoint-compatible, merged).
Links: https://vllm.ai/blog/2026-05-26-eagle-3-1 (fetched this session).
Why it is OUR find: this is a named, mechanistic diagnosis of **exactly our F-235 deep-position deficit** (53.1 % deep vs 93.6 % shallow acceptance, SVG-CUSTOM.md:8074-8109). Our drafter IS the recursive form — `capture_step` takes the previous step's residual + token (`src/core/mtp.cpp:1366-1391`, T26-2 §a.3) — and its input path fuses the anchor's 4-stream R through `hc_norm` + `input_mix_weight_down/up` (`src/core/mtp.cpp:1199-1223`, T26-2 §c.6). Both fixes are trainable-side, export-shape-preserving if the norm is folded at rt build (F-109 double-fold law). No new tensors ⇒ no pack-contract break.

**A2. Draft-OPD — on-policy distillation with error-position replay (May 28, 2026).**
arXiv:2605.29343. Mechanism: (i) target-assisted rollouts where each draft block's start is an "anchor"; (ii) **error-position replay** — from each anchor, replay the drafter's own proposed block (including positions REJECTED at verify) and compute student+teacher log-probs on the drafter-induced prefix; (iii) accept/reject-aware objective — forward KL on accepted positions, reverse KL on rejected, position-decay weights w_k = γ^(k−1), γ=0.8. Reported vs EAGLE-3 **+23 %**, vs DFlash **+13 %** at matched FLOPs (Qwen3-4B/8B/30B-A3B, H200); SGLang serving τ ≈ +11 % [donor-reported]. Lossless.
Links: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.29343v1 (fetched).
Why it fits: our engine already localizes rejection positions for free — the exact-match walk `a` and the rescue path (`src/program/generate.cpp:15384-15413`, read this session) and the per-depth `dp_tested/dp_ok` telemetry (:15416-15417). The T14A exec-A dump already walks prefill teacher-forced; an OPD harvest v2 = add rejection-anchor records to the SAME dump lane. This is the mission's "replay/on-policy data" item, and it is the strongest of the class.

**A3. EDR — Expected Decoding Rounds objective with exact gradient (Oct 7, 2026).**
arXiv:2610.10411 (two weeks old). Models speculative decoding as a Markov reward process, derives the **Expected Decoding Rounds objective and its exact gradient**, plus an offline EDR evaluator counting expected rounds from target outputs. Aimed at parallel/semi-AR drafters; the OBJECTIVE is cost-weighted acceptance — the trained form of the c_row arithmetic T14C derived by hand (0.72 tokens/row rule).
Links: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10411 (fetched).
Caveat: no independent replication yet; applies cleanly to any drafter including our recursive head. [estimated] +0.5–1.5 % M1 as a T14B objective rider, cost M (trainer objective + offline evaluator; the evaluator maps onto our planned replay harness). Same G0.2 falsifier as every other rider.

**A4. Periphery of the training lane (mapped, not riders).** LK-line follow-up "Halfway Speculative Decoding" (OpenReview NLdAp0Oizb, Sep 29 2026 — bot-blocked page; listing-level only): https://openreview.net/forum?id=NLdAp0Oizb . Live/continuous draft training (Baseten, Jun 2026, +20 % median accept [donor-reported]): https://www.baseten.co/blog/live-draft-model-training-for-speculative-decoding — violates our one-variable/five-boot/quality-gate methodology on-box; not a candidate. ### B. Offer-side: keep/drop rules, confidence scheduling, depth adaptation

**B1. vLLM Dynamic Speculation Length RFC #36657 (opened Mar 10, 2026; closed not-planned Jul 27, 2026).**
Mechanism: `draft_confidence_threshold` τ (default 0 = off); after each draft step, halt drafting when the **batch-mean** top softmax prob < τ; async 1-byte D2H flag on a side stream; zero-pad to fixed [B,K]. Recommended τ 0.4–0.6. Future work named in the Mar 26 2026 design doc: a **DraftLengthPolicy abstraction** and **device-side control flow** (torch.while_loop).
Links: https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/36657 (fetched).
Read: the largest serving stack proposed, then shelved, exactly the door our flat gate already occupies per-draft (`generate.cpp:15162-15164`) and that draft_cum+conditional-graph would complete (their "device-side control flow" future work = our §c.5 conditional graph). Nothing to adopt; strong external confirmation that offer-side shaping is where the field converged (T25-2 Implication 2).

**B2. AdaSD — entropy + Jensen-Shannon adaptive stopping AND acceptance (Dec 12, 2025; v2 May 26, 2026).**
arXiv:2512.11280. Up to 1.46× over vanilla SD, **with accuracy loss up to 1.8 %** — the acceptance-rule half is NOT lossless. Links: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.11280 (fetched). → negative candidate (see (d)).

**B3. Adaptive-depth confirmations.** llama.cpp discussion #23738 (May 26, 2026): adaptive `--spec-draft-n-max` from rolling acceptance + context length; MTP=2 beats MTP=1, MTP=3 noisier at long context on Apple Silicon; cites PEARL (arXiv:2408.11850), AdaSD, TALON, SpecKV (arXiv:2605.02888) as the fixed-params-are-suboptimal literature. Links: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/discussions/23738 (fetched), https://github.com/dmitryryabkov/mtp-profiler . TapOut bandit depth (arXiv:2511.02017, list-level): https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.02017 . All confirm the door; none beats a refit joint rule on cost (bandits/rolling rates are noisier estimators than our per-draft dprob + EMA adapt).

**D2. DynaSpec — context-routed drafting shortlist (Oct 11 2025; v3 Feb 3 2026).**
arXiv:2510.13847 (fetched). Meta-classifiers route each context to token clusters; union = per-context shortlist; routing overlapped with drafting. Recovers **98.4 % of full-vocab mean accepted length vs 93.6 % for static shortlists**; up to 2.23× vs 1.91× throughput on rare-token corpora; lossless.
Read for us: (i) it prices the AL cost of a STATIC subset (~6 % of AL on rare-token corpora) — our EN subset is static, so a small acceptance cost is plausible on out-of-domain prose [estimated, no number transferable]; (ii) but the mechanism's motivation (O(V·d) head projection) is absent on our head — sub-vocab 40,525 rows, select kernel is one thread + small dots (`verify_kernels.cu:671-686`, read this session). A DynaSpec-style routed subset is a far-future T14X training rider at best, priced behind every T14B/T14C item.

## (d) Negative candidates — look strong, die against exact-match + c_row + bit-identity

1. **AdaSD** (arXiv:2512.11280) — the closest-looking: adaptive stopping AND acceptance "hyperparameter-free". Dies twice: its acceptance-rule half sacrifices exactness (≤1.8 % accuracy loss, by its own report) — fatal under bit-identity/quality gates; its stopping half uses entropy + JSD, which are strictly weaker signals than our dprob under exact-match (dprob IS the per-draft acceptance probability; F-228's gate already consumes it). Top negative.
2. **DSpine / DFlash2-as-replacement / SpecFormer / TiDAR / SpecDiff-2 / AdaFlash drafter** — the parallel/separate-drafter class. Three independent kills: (i) a separate model breaks the coupled 31-tensor rt contract (deliberate pack v2 territory at T14C+, and none of these is a 12,800-feat head — they are 0.6–1B models); (ii) SGLang/Hopper-row economics — DSpine's own numbers cap the class at ~+4 % AL over DFlash2 [donor-reported], and their block-4 offered width is BELOW our S_mtp; (iii) c_row law: our rows are 26–30 % of window vs their ~17 % (T17C §1.6), so their AL gains clear our 0.72-token break-even even less often. The salvage is training-side only: layer-wise supervision / early-forming predictions (DSpine), anti-decay convs (DFlash2) → T14B trainer notes.
3. **TALON adaptive trees** (arXiv:2601.07353) — trees remain dead by arithmetic: the hedge pays only at per-position acceptance m < ~0.6, and T14B-class training raises m ABOVE that (T17C §1.3). Adaptive tree SHAPE is a better allocator over a structure that still buys full rows for hedges.
4. **P-EAGLE / MineDraft / SGLang Spec-V2 overlap / H-Spec** — batch-serving scheduling (parallel proposals, overlap sync removal). No single-stream door exists for these; the M1 lane's spec-side doors are exhausted (T25-2 Implication 2); the conc lane has its own priced ladder (T26-4). H-Spec's "parallel drafting without a drafter" (arXiv:2609.24197) is multi-proposal per position — ≤1 survivor per row under exact-match (T17C §1.4).
5. **SelfJudge / DIVERSED** — acceptance-relaxing verification (arXiv:2510.02329, arXiv:2604.07622). Non-exact by design. Dead by law; F-235's −4.2 % is the measured size of that door's even-lossless version on our stack.
6. **DynaSpec context-routed draft vocab** — lossless and confirmed effective, but its O(V·d) head-projection motivation does not exist on our sub-vocab head (microsecond select, `verify_kernels.cu:671-686`); routed routing adds a classifier + training rider for a gain our anatomy cannot express. Far-future T14X note only.
7. **Baseten live draft training** — continuous on-box adaptation violates one-variable, five-boot means, and quality-gate methodology. No.
