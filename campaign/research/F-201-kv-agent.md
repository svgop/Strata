# Deep brief 5: KV cache (investigative-researcher, 2026-10-15) — 10 findings

Framing: the 4.40ms attention line is LATENCY-bound (per-step gather ~1MB at top-k 3 × page 4 × 12KB × ≤6 rows — 4.4ms for 1MB = pointer-chase latency, not bandwidth).

1. **int4 KV via token-wise per-head scales + K-only block-128 Hadamard (SAW-INT4, arXiv:2604.19157)** — the ONLY int4 family compatible with paged layouts (channel-wise/codebooks/PCA break coalescing). Naive int4 collapses (Qwen3-8B 70.84→0.00!) but K-only rotation recovers (69.97); fused rotation costs 0.10-0.28% prefill / ~0 decode. H100 serving: +10.7% TPS @conc 8, +41.4% @64. For us: KV lines halve on a latency-bound gather → 1.25-1.8× on the 4.40ms line; L2 covers 16k int4 tokens vs 8k. ADOPT (gate at depth 110k).
2. **Delta-only re-admission** (parked images in FINAL streaming layout; 55-58GB/s pinned H2D effective on 5090): a 1-4k-token turn delta = 0.2-0.9ms wire vs 24ms full (our 30-56ms) — **10-30× faster admission**; Spin (arXiv:2604.26837) lets GPU kernels read host-pinned metadata directly (zero-copy page tables). ADOPT.
3. **UNIQUE (arXiv:2605.27740)**: score pages by q·mean_p + 0.5‖q‖·std_p with per-page stats computed ONCE and cached across steps (no per-step key reads in the scorer); top-k = two-round 8-bit radix select (2× FlashInfer top_k, 0.013ms at P=1024); 11.4× vs dense at 32k, 97% quality, page 8. Est. 0.5-1.5ms off our line incl. the serial indexer slot. ADOPT stat-cache + radix.
4. **In-graph KV-gather overlap with GDN/expert compute** (ProMoE pattern, arXiv:2410.22134: 2.07× avg decode): the gather is independent of expert routing — concurrent graph branch, event dependency only before the QSA layer. Hides nearly all of 4.40ms behind the ~20-26ms non-attention window → window 25-31 → ~21-27ms. ADOPT (pure scheduling).
5. **Page 8 beats 4 on BOTH axes** (vAttention: paged decode good at 16-32, degrades ≥64; Han Lab SNR: halving B hurts like halving head dim, B=32→16 top-2 acc 47→21.5%; UNIQUE+Spin both chose 8): ~1.1-1.4× gather (half the block-table loads), 27.5k→13.7k indexer entries at 110k, 1.4× selection SNR. A/B 4 vs 8.
6. **Cross-layer index sharing (TidalDecode, ICLR'25)**: adjacent-layer selections highly overlap; 2 selection layers + reuse = 2.17× vs per-layer Quest, 2.1× e2e decode (A100). Halves indexer invocations; ~3-4× indexer work cut combined with the 35-50% step overlap. PILOT with recall gate.
7. **FP4 KV decode is production-grade on OUR GPU** (FlashMLA Blackwell FP4/FP8 decode path; SageAttention3: FP4 attention 1038 TOPS on RTX 5090, 5× vs FA): mine scale-layout/TMA-gather/dequant-schedule patterns (MLA shape differs — don't port the kernel).
8. **Static indexes ROT — incremental maintenance mandatory (ParisKV, arXiv:2602.07721)**: coarse Recall@100 collapses to 6% without refresh; append-only scoring for new pages + windowed (every 32-64 steps) partial re-rank; 16.9× vs MagicPIG at 1M ctx. ADOPT the maintenance policy.
9. **L2 persistence carveout: measured WEAK (1.00× uncontended, 1.16× best mixed)** — SKIP; our banked cp.async.bulk.prefetch.L2 covers the same span. (Only revisit if querying cudaDevAttrMaxPersistingL2CacheSize on Blackwell shows headroom.)
10. **Hierarchical parking validated** (LMCache 50GB/rank host tier; SwiftCache cross-session −69% P99; NVMe 14.5GB/s cold tier): roadmap only.

**Sequence: graph overlap (zero risk) → int4 swap (rotation gate) → stat-cache+radix scorer → page 8 A/B → delta re-admission → cross-layer sharing → incremental maintenance. Target: 4.40ms → ~1.5-2.5ms; window 25-31 → ~20-24ms.**
